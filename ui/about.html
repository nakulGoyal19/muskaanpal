About Muskaan

Fashion, Beauty & Lifestyle creator based in India — sharing what I love with people who care about style.

Hey, I'm Muskaan!

I'm a fashion, beauty, and lifestyle content creator from India. What started as a passion for sharing my everyday outfits and makeup looks with friends has grown into a community of people who love discovering new products, styling ideas, and honest reviews — just like I do.

I create content on both YouTube and Instagram, where I share everything from budget-friendly fashion hauls and bridal makeup tutorials to perfume recommendations and lifestyle vlogs. My goal is simple: help you look and feel your best without breaking the bank.

What I Create

My content spans across several categories, all rooted in things I genuinely use and enjoy:

  • Fashion Hauls & Styling: I love putting together outfits from affordable brands and showing how to style them for different occasions — from casual everyday looks to festive and wedding-ready outfits.
  • Makeup & Beauty: Whether it's a full glam bridal look or a quick five-minute everyday routine, I share tutorials, product reviews, and tips that actually work.
  • Perfume Picks & Dupes: Fragrance is a huge passion of mine. I hunt down affordable dupes for luxury perfumes and share my honest take on what's worth buying — from designer scents to hidden gems you can find on Amazon and Myntra.
  • Lifestyle & Vlogs: Day-in-my-life content, shopping trips, travel diaries, and behind-the-scenes moments that keep things real and relatable.

Why I Started

I've always believed that great style doesn't have to come with a hefty price tag. Growing up, I loved experimenting with fashion and beauty, and I realised there was a gap — not enough creators were talking about affordable alternatives that actually deliver. That's what pushed me to start sharing my finds online.

Today, I'm grateful to have built a space where thousands of people come for honest recommendations. Every product I feature on my website is something I've personally tried. I never promote anything I wouldn't use myself — that's a promise.

About This Website

muskaanpal.in is my personal hub where you can find all my favourite product picks, watch my latest videos, and shop the exact items I recommend — all in one place. I use affiliate links from Amazon, Myntra, and other trusted retailers, which means if you purchase through my links, I may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. It's one of the ways that helps me keep creating content for you.

Whether you're here for perfume dupes, fashion inspiration, or just a good vlog to watch — I'm happy you stopped by. Thanks for being part of this journey with me!
